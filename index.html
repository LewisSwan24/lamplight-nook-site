Lamplight Nook

A cozy pixel shop where a small team does computer jobs for you, powered by Claude.

Coming soon to Microsoft StoreUS$9.99, one-time. Windows 10/11.

Hand a commission slip to the counter and the staff get to work. Wick plans the job, Clem runs out to look things up, Iris settles the colours and layout, Flint checks everything before it leaves the shop, and Chestnut the cat supervises. Behind the pixels it is Claude Code, Anthropic's AI agent, working in a folder on your PC, and every step shows up as life in the shop.

Ask for real things

A small web page, a researched summary, an edited copy of a document, a plan for a messy folder.

Watch them work

Errands, chalkboard notes and checks instead of logs.

You stay in charge

Wick asks before choices; risky steps wait for your OK. Ring the bell to add a request mid-job.

It remembers you

Past jobs, your choices and the snags you hit, kept only on your PC.

A shop that lives

Weather and seasons from the calendar, holiday decorations, a back garden with Malt the dog and the hens, and neighbours who drop in.

What you need

  • Claude Code on your PC (free; the app walks you through installing it).
  • Your own Anthropic API key. You pay Anthropic for what the staff use, usually well under a dollar for a small job. Every job shows its cost, and you can set a cap.
  • No account, no subscription, no telemetry. Not available where Anthropic does not offer its API, including mainland China and Hong Kong.
